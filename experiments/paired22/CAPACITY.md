remain immutable and cannot be resumed or rerun. This directory does not define
an admitted scientific protocol or runnable workflow.

192 MiB and the terminal reserve remains 512 KiB. The cap prevents unbounded
evidence growth; the focused test proves the observed paired21 failure prefix
has room for another similarly sized decision under this limit. It does not
prove that any new batch will complete. A future fixed batch needs fresh seeds,
its own runner/source pins, whole-artifact and multi-copy resource forecast,
reviews, and a separate exact-head admission before CI execution.
